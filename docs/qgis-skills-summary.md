# QGIS Skills Summary

I bring a software engineering mindset to GIS work: I document assumptions, keep inputs and outputs organized, and check workflows for edge cases before treating results as final. That translates well to QGIS support, workflow documentation, QA, and AI-output review.

- Software engineering background that supports structured data tasks and reproducible process design
- Data workflow thinking with consistent naming, source tracking, and separation of raw versus derived outputs
- Documentation quality with clear step ordering, concise notes, and explicit limitations
- Technical review with projection checks, unit awareness, and validation of spatial joins, buffers, and overlays
- Reproducibility through enough detail for another person to rerun the workflow in QGIS
- Attention to edge cases such as missing attributes, geometry problems, CRS mismatch, and incomplete coverage
- Ability to evaluate AI outputs by identifying unsupported claims, vague instructions, and missing QA before following generated GIS guidance
